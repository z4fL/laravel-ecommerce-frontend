# Customer Service Assistant

<!-- impeccable:surface-brief 1 -->

## Purpose & Scope
A persistent, floating customer service assistant accessible across the storefront. It provides read-only support for orders (status, line items, order totals), payment status, and shipping addresses by querying the existing backend API, adhering strictly to the contract in `docs/api-docs.yaml`.

## Mode
Operate

## Direction contract

### THESIS
A quiet, index-style commerce concierge embedded in The Shop's flat charcoal/bone world. It rejects fake chatbot typing loops, hallucinations, and rounded playful bubble widgets in favor of structured data receipts, clear status pills, precise query suggestions, and honest capability boundaries.

### OWN-WORLD
Inherits The Shop's spectrum-index aesthetic: square corners (0px radius), charcoal header, bone/paper reading surface, fine 1px neutral rules, Archivo typography, and spectral-citron / marker-olive accents for active signals. No rounded bubbles, no floating drop-shadow halos.

### STORY
Shoppers easily look up recent orders, review payment requirements, check shipping addresses, or receive clear guidance on what the self-service assistant can and cannot do without leaving their current shopping flow.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
Fixed bottom-right trigger (charcoal square button with spectral-citron icon, 52px, 0px radius). When opened: a 380px wide × 560px tall panel (or full viewport bottom-sheet on mobile <640px) featuring a clean header with status indicator, a structured message stream, quick suggestion tags, an inquiry composer with instant action buttons, and clear state transitions for loading, error, empty, and retry.

### FORM
Direct extension of The Shop's existing design system and storefront layout.

### FINISH
unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
